# Annual Return in Hong Kong

## Overview
Every Hong Kong company must file an Annual Return with the Companies Registry each year. The Annual Return (Form NAR1) provides up-to-date information about the company's key details.

## Legal Requirement
- Required under Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622), Section 662.
- Must be filed within 42 days of the company's return date (anniversary of incorporation).
- Failure to file is a criminal offence and incurs higher fees.

## Annual Return Date
- The return date is the anniversary of the company's date of incorporation.
- Example: If incorporated on 15 March 2020, the annual return date each year is 15 March.

## Information in the Annual Return (Form NAR1)
The Annual Return captures the position of the company as at the return date, including:
1. Registered office address.
2. Principal place of business (if different).
3. Details of company secretary.
4. Details of all directors.
5. Issued share capital and list of members (shareholders).
6. Mortgage/charge register (whether any charges exist).
7. Whether the company has passed a resolution to dispense with AGM (if applicable).

## Filing Fee
- Filed within 42 days: HKD 105 (online) or HKD 140 (paper).
- Filed late (after 42 days but within 3 months of return date): HKD 870.
- Filed late (3–6 months after return date): HKD 1,740.
- Filed late (6–9 months after return date): HKD 2,610.
- Filed late (more than 9 months after return date): HKD 3,480.

## How to File
1. **Online (eCR portal)**: Fastest and cheapest method. Digital signature of director or company secretary required.
2. **Paper**: Download Form NAR1, complete, sign by director or company secretary, and submit to Companies Registry by post or in person.

## Consequences of Non-Filing
- Company and every responsible person (directors, company secretary) commit an offence.
- Fine of HKD 50,000 and a daily default fine of HKD 700.
- Companies Registry may take action to strike off the company.

## Relationship to Business Registration Certificate
- The Business Registration Certificate (BRC) must also be renewed annually (or every 3 years).
- Renewal notice is sent by the Inland Revenue Department approximately 1 month before expiry.
- BRC renewal fee: HKD 2,150 per year (as of 2024).

## Tips for Company Secretaries
- Diarise the return date and filing deadline (return date + 42 days).
- Update all company details before the return date to ensure accuracy.
- Use the eCR portal for faster processing and lower fees.
- Retain proof of filing for company records.

## Relevant Legislation
- Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622), Sections 662–666
