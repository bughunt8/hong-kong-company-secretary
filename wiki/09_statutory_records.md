# Statutory Records and Registers in Hong Kong

## Overview
Every Hong Kong company must maintain a comprehensive set of statutory records and registers. These records must be kept up-to-date, retained for specified periods, and made available for inspection by relevant parties.

## Required Statutory Registers
A Hong Kong company must maintain the following registers:

### 1. Register of Members
- Names and addresses of all current and past shareholders.
- Number and class of shares held by each member.
- Dates of entry and cessation.
- Public document – available for inspection by any person.

### 2. Register of Directors
- Full name and residential/correspondence address of each director.
- Identity card/passport details.
- Date of appointment and any cessation.
- Public document.

### 3. Register of Company Secretaries
- Name and address of the company secretary (individual or corporate).
- Date of appointment and cessation.
- Public document.

### 4. Register of Charges
- Details of all mortgages and charges created by the company.
- Must be updated when charges are created or satisfied.
- Public document.

### 5. Significant Controllers Register (SCR)
- Details of individuals who ultimately own or control 25% or more of the company, or who otherwise exercise significant control.
- Required since March 2018 (Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing (Financial Institutions) Ordinance amendments).
- Must be kept at the registered office or SAIL.
- Accessible to law enforcement upon request; not publicly available.

### 6. Register of Debenture Holders (if applicable)
- Required if the company has issued debentures.

## Minutes and Resolutions
- Minutes of all board meetings and general meetings must be recorded and kept.
- Written resolutions must also be maintained.
- Retained for at least 10 years.

## Annual Return (Form NAR1)
- Filed annually; a copy kept in company records.

## Financial Records
- Accounting records must be kept for at least 7 years.
- Must show all transactions and the financial position of the company.

## Location of Registers
- Kept at the registered office or at a Specified Alternative Inspection Location (SAIL) in Hong Kong.
- A SAIL must be registered with Companies Registry (Form NR2).

## Inspection Rights
| Register | Who Can Inspect | Fee |
|---|---|---|
| Register of Members | Any person | Free (or small fee) |
| Register of Directors | Any person | Free (or small fee) |
| Register of Charges | Any person | Free (or small fee) |
| Significant Controllers Register | Law enforcement only | N/A |
| Minutes of Board Meetings | Directors only | N/A |
| Minutes of General Meetings | Members | Free |

## Penalties for Non-Compliance
- Failure to maintain registers: criminal offence, fines up to HKD 300,000.
- Failure to allow inspection: criminal offence.

## Relevant Legislation
- Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622), Parts 2, 8, 14
- Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance (Cap. 615)
