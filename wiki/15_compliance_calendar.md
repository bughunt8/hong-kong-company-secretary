# Compliance Calendar for Hong Kong Companies

## Overview
This article provides a summary of the key compliance deadlines and recurring obligations for a Hong Kong private limited company throughout the year.

## Annual Recurring Obligations

### Monthly / As Needed
- **Employer's Returns for departing employees**: File Form IR56F/IR56G with IRD within 1 month of departure or end of employment.

### Within 15 Days of Change
- **Change of director**: File Form ND2A with Companies Registry.
- **Change of company secretary**: File Form ND2A with Companies Registry.
- **Change of registered office**: File Form NR1 with Companies Registry.

### Within 30 Days
- **Stamp duty on share transfers**: Submit to Stamp Duty Office within 30 days of execution.

### Within 42 Days of Anniversary of Incorporation
- **Annual Return (Form NAR1)**: File with Companies Registry. Late filing incurs significantly higher fees.

### Annual – Business Registration
- **Renew Business Registration Certificate**: IRD sends renewal notice ~1 month before expiry. 1-year or 3-year renewal options available.

### Annual – Tax Filing
- **Profits Tax Return**: Filed annually upon receipt from IRD. New companies receive first return ~18 months after incorporation.
- **Employer's Annual Return (Form IR56B)**: Filed by 1 May each year for all employees' income in the preceding year ended 31 March.

### Annual – AGM (if applicable)
- **Annual General Meeting**: Required for public companies; optional for private companies that have dispensed with AGM by resolution.

## Significant Controllers Register Updates
- Must be updated within **7 days** of any change in significant controllers.
- Annual review recommended to ensure SCR remains accurate.

## Key Deadlines Summary Table

| Obligation | Trigger / Deadline | Form | Authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Annual Return | Within 42 days of incorporation anniversary | NAR1 | Companies Registry |
| Director change | Within 15 days of change | ND2A | Companies Registry |
| Company secretary change | Within 15 days of change | ND2A | Companies Registry |
| Registered office change | Within 15 days of change | NR1 | Companies Registry |
| Business Registration renewal | Before BRC expiry date | — | IRD |
| Share transfer stamp duty | Within 30 days of execution | — | Stamp Duty Office |
| Profits Tax Return | Upon receipt (usually Apr–Nov) | BIR51/BIR52 | IRD |
| Employer's Annual Return | By 1 May each year | IR56B | IRD |
| AGM (public companies) | Within 6 months of financial year end | — | — |

## New Company Checklist (First Year)
- [ ] Obtain Certificate of Incorporation and Business Registration Certificate.
- [ ] Open a corporate bank account.
- [ ] Set up accounting records and choose a financial year-end.
- [ ] Appoint an auditor (required for all Hong Kong companies with a Profits Tax obligation).
- [ ] Maintain Significant Controllers Register.
- [ ] Diarise Annual Return due date (incorporation anniversary + 42 days).
- [ ] File Annual Return in first year (Form NAR1).
- [ ] Register for eTAX with IRD for online filing convenience.

## Penalties Overview
- Late Annual Return: HKD 870 – HKD 3,480 (depending on how late).
- Failure to file Profits Tax Return: fines and potential prosecution.
- Failure to renew BRC: fines and potential prosecution.
- Non-compliance with SCR: fines up to HKD 25,000.
