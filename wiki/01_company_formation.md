# Company Formation in Hong Kong

## Overview
Hong Kong is one of the easiest places in the world to incorporate a company. A private limited company (Ltd) can typically be registered within 1–3 business days.

## Types of Hong Kong Companies
- **Private Company Limited by Shares** – Most common structure for businesses. Limited liability, shares cannot be offered to the public.
- **Public Company Limited by Shares** – Can offer shares to the public; subject to stricter disclosure requirements.
- **Company Limited by Guarantee** – Typically used by non-profit organisations.
- **Unlimited Company** – Rare; members have unlimited liability.
- **Branch Office** – A foreign company operating in Hong Kong as an extension of its overseas entity.

## Requirements for Incorporation
1. **Company Name** – Must be unique, end in "Limited" (or "有限公司" in Chinese). Check availability via the Companies Registry eCR system.
2. **Shareholders** – Minimum 1, maximum 50 (for private company). Can be individuals or corporate entities of any nationality.
3. **Directors** – Minimum 1 natural person director of any nationality. No residency requirement.
4. **Company Secretary** – Must be Hong Kong resident (individual) or a company incorporated in Hong Kong.
5. **Registered Office** – Must be a physical address in Hong Kong (P.O. Box not allowed).
6. **Share Capital** – No minimum paid-up capital requirement. Typical issued share capital is HKD 10,000 or USD 1.

## Incorporation Process
1. Reserve company name (optional, done via eCR portal).
2. Prepare Incorporation Form (NNC1 for company limited by shares).
3. Prepare Articles of Association.
4. Submit to Companies Registry along with the fee (HKD 1,720 for standard processing).
5. Obtain Certificate of Incorporation (CI) and Business Registration Certificate (BRC) simultaneously.

## Key Documents
- **Certificate of Incorporation (CI)** – Issued by Companies Registry, proves legal existence.
- **Business Registration Certificate (BRC)** – Issued by Inland Revenue Department, required to operate legally.
- **Articles of Association** – Governs internal management of the company.

## Timeline
- Online submission via eCR: 1–2 business days
- Paper submission: 3–7 business days
- Same-day service available for an additional fee (HKD 280 extra)

## Costs
> ⚠️ Fees are subject to change. Always verify current amounts on the Companies Registry and IRD websites before filing.

- Companies Registry incorporation fee: HKD 1,720
- Business Registration Certificate (1 year): HKD 2,150
- Business Registration Certificate (3 years): HKD 5,950

## Relevant Legislation
- Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622)
- Business Registration Ordinance (Cap. 310)
