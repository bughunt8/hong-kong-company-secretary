# Registered Office in Hong Kong

## Overview
Every Hong Kong company must have a registered office address in Hong Kong at all times. The registered office is the official address for correspondence, service of legal documents, and inspection of statutory records.

## Legal Requirements
- Must be a physical address in Hong Kong (not a P.O. Box).
- Must be accessible during normal business hours for document service.
- The registered office address is a matter of public record.
- Required under Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622), Section 93.

## Functions of the Registered Office
1. Receives official correspondence from government authorities (Companies Registry, Inland Revenue Department, etc.).
2. Address for service of legal notices and documents.
3. Location where statutory registers must be kept and made available for inspection (unless a SAIL – Specified Alternative Inspection Location – is used).
4. Displayed on company's letterheads, invoices, and official communications.

## Changing the Registered Office
- File Form NR1 with the Companies Registry within 15 days of the change.
- The new address must also be in Hong Kong.
- Fee: HKD 295 per notification.

## Virtual Office / Registered Office Services
Many professional firms offer registered office address services, which include:
- Use of their office address as the company's registered office.
- Mail forwarding and scanning services.
- Handling of correspondence from government bodies.
- Typical cost: HKD 2,000–5,000 per year.

## Specified Alternative Inspection Location (SAIL)
- Companies may maintain statutory registers at a SAIL other than the registered office.
- The SAIL must also be in Hong Kong.
- Register the SAIL by filing Form NR2.

## Obligations Related to Registered Office
- The registered office must be maintained at all times; there can be no gap in the address.
- If the company is wound up or dissolved, the registered office remains relevant for legal service during the dissolution process.

## Relevant Legislation
- Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622), Sections 93–96
