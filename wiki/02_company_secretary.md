# Company Secretary in Hong Kong

## Overview
Every Hong Kong company must have a company secretary at all times. The company secretary plays a crucial administrative and compliance role, acting as the liaison between the company and regulatory authorities.

## Legal Requirement
Under the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622), every company incorporated in Hong Kong must appoint a company secretary within a reasonable time after incorporation. Failure to have a company secretary is a criminal offence.

## Who Can Be a Company Secretary?
- **Individual**: Must be ordinarily resident in Hong Kong (holds a Hong Kong ID card or has the right of abode/right to land).
- **Corporate body**: Must be a company incorporated in Hong Kong with a registered office in Hong Kong.
- **Restriction**: The sole director of a private company cannot also be the company secretary.

## Key Responsibilities
1. **Annual Return filing** – File Form NAR1 with the Companies Registry within 42 days of the anniversary of incorporation.
2. **Maintain statutory registers** – Including Register of Members, Register of Directors, Register of Company Secretaries, Register of Charges, Significant Controllers Register.
3. **Organise board meetings** – Prepare agendas, take minutes, distribute resolutions.
4. **Organise Annual General Meeting (AGM)** – For companies required to hold one.
5. **File statutory forms** – Notify Companies Registry of any changes (directors, address, share capital, etc.).
6. **Ensure compliance** – Keep company informed of legal obligations under Companies Ordinance.
7. **Maintain company seal** (if the company has one).
8. **Safeguard statutory books and records**.

## Appointment and Removal
- Appointment is recorded in the Incorporation Form (NNC1) or via Form ND2A after incorporation.
- Changes must be filed with Companies Registry within 15 days using Form ND2A.
- The company secretary's particulars must appear in the Annual Return.

## Liability
The company secretary is not personally liable for the company's debts, but may be liable for:
- Criminal penalties for failing to file required forms on time.
- Civil liability if negligence causes loss.

## Professional Qualifications
While not legally required by the Companies Ordinance, many company secretaries hold qualifications from:
- The Hong Kong Institute of Chartered Secretaries (HKICS)
- The Chartered Governance Institute (CGI/ICSA)

## Fees (Typical Market Rates)
- Professional company secretarial services: HKD 3,000–10,000 per year for small private companies.
