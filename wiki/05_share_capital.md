# Share Capital and Shareholders in Hong Kong

## Overview
A Hong Kong private company limited by shares is owned by its shareholders. The company's share capital structure defines the ownership and rights of shareholders.

## Share Capital Basics
- **Authorised Share Capital**: Hong Kong abolished this concept under the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622, 2014). Companies no longer have a maximum authorised share capital.
- **Issued/Paid-up Share Capital**: The shares actually issued to shareholders.
- **Minimum**: No minimum paid-up capital requirement.
- **Typical**: HKD 10,000 divided into 10,000 ordinary shares of HKD 1 each, or 1 share of USD 1.

## Types of Shares
- **Ordinary Shares** – Standard shares with voting rights and right to dividends.
- **Preference Shares** – Priority on dividends and/or return of capital; may be non-voting.
- **Redeemable Shares** – Can be bought back by the company.
- **Treasury Shares** – Shares bought back and held by the company (permitted under Cap. 622).

## Shareholders
- **Minimum**: 1 shareholder (private company can be a single-member company).
- **Maximum**: 50 members for a private company (excluding employee shareholders).
- **Nationality**: No restrictions – shareholders can be of any nationality and reside anywhere.
- **Corporate Shareholders**: Companies (local or foreign) can be shareholders.

## Register of Members
- Must be maintained showing: names and addresses of members, number and class of shares held, dates of entry.
- Available for public inspection.
- Must be kept at the registered office or SAIL.

## Share Transfers
- Shares of a private company are typically restricted from free transfer (per Articles of Association).
- Transfer instrument: Share Transfer Form (stock transfer form).
- Board approval usually required.
- Stamp Duty: 0.2% of the higher of consideration or market value (0.1% paid by each party).
- File return with Stamp Duty Office within 30 days.

## Allotment of New Shares
- Board resolution to allot shares (or shareholder approval if pre-emption rights apply).
- File Return of Allotment (Form NSC1) with Companies Registry within 1 month.

## Dividends
- Declared by directors (interim dividend) or members (final dividend by ordinary resolution).
- Cannot exceed distributable profits.
- No withholding tax on dividends in Hong Kong.

## Shareholder Rights
- Right to receive dividends.
- Right to attend and vote at general meetings.
- Right to inspect statutory records.
- Right to receive annual reports and accounts.
- Right to appoint proxies.
- Minority shareholder protections available under Companies Ordinance.

## Relevant Legislation
- Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622), Parts 4, 5, 12
- Stamp Duty Ordinance (Cap. 117)
