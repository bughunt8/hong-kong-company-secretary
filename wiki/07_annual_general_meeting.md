# Annual General Meeting (AGM) in Hong Kong

## Overview
An Annual General Meeting (AGM) is a formal meeting of a company's shareholders held each year. Under the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622), certain companies are required to hold AGMs.

## Which Companies Must Hold an AGM?
- **Public companies** – Must hold an AGM every year.
- **Private companies** – May elect to dispense with AGMs by passing a written resolution.
- A private company that has passed a resolution to dispense with AGMs does not need to hold one unless requested by shareholders holding at least 5% of voting rights.

## Timeline for AGMs
- A public company must hold its first AGM within 18 months of incorporation.
- Subsequent AGMs must be held within 6 months of the end of the company's financial year and no more than 15 months after the previous AGM.

## Business Conducted at an AGM
Typical AGM business (often referred to as "ordinary business") includes:
1. Receiving and adopting the annual financial statements and director's report.
2. Declaring a final dividend (if any).
3. Electing/re-electing directors (if required by Articles).
4. Appointing or re-appointing auditors and fixing their remuneration.
5. Any other business notified in the agenda.

## Notice Requirements
- **Public companies**: At least 21 days' written notice for an AGM.
- **Private companies**: At least 14 days' notice (unless Articles require longer notice).
- Notice must be sent to all members, directors, and auditors.
- Notice must state the place, date, time, and the agenda.

## Quorum
- Typically 2 members (or 1 member if the company has only 1 member).
- Specific quorum requirements are set out in the Articles of Association.

## Resolutions
- **Ordinary Resolution**: Passed by simple majority (more than 50% of votes cast).
- **Special Resolution**: Requires at least 75% majority; needed for significant changes (amend Articles, change name, reduce capital, etc.).
- Resolutions may be passed by show of hands or by poll.

## Written Resolutions (for Private Companies)
- Private companies may pass written resolutions instead of holding meetings.
- All members entitled to vote must sign the written resolution.
- Useful for routine matters when a formal meeting is not necessary.

## Minutes
- Minutes of all general meetings must be maintained.
- Kept at the registered office or SAIL.
- Accessible to members upon request.

## Dispensing with AGMs (Private Companies)
- Pass an elective resolution (written resolution by all members) to dispense with AGMs.
- Effect lasts until revoked by any member.
- Company must still circulate annual accounts to members.

## Relevant Legislation
- Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622), Divisions 1–5 of Part 12 (Sections 565–613)
