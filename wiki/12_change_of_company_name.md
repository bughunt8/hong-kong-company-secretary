# Change of Company Name in Hong Kong

## Overview
A Hong Kong company can change its name at any time by passing a special resolution and filing the appropriate forms with the Companies Registry.

## Process for Changing Company Name

### Step 1: Check Name Availability
- The new name must not be identical or too similar to an existing registered company name.
- Check name availability via the Companies Registry's eCR system or by writing to the Registrar.
- Names are subject to approval by the Registrar of Companies.

### Step 2: Pass a Special Resolution
- Shareholders must pass a Special Resolution (at least 75% majority) to approve the name change.
- The resolution must specify the new proposed name.
- Alternatively, the Articles of Association may permit the board to change the name.

### Step 3: File Form NNC2 with Companies Registry
- Submit Form NNC2 (Notice of Change of Company Name) together with a certified copy of the Special Resolution.
- Pay the prescribed fee: HKD 295.

### Step 4: Receive Certificate of Change of Name
- The Companies Registry issues a Certificate of Change of Name upon approval.
- The name change is effective from the date on the Certificate.

## Timeline
- Online submission: typically 1–2 business days.
- Paper submission: 3–7 business days.

## After the Name Change
The company must update its name on:
1. Business Registration Certificate – Notify IRD and obtain updated BRC.
2. Company seal (if the company uses one).
3. Letterheads, invoices, contracts, and official documents.
4. Bank account records – Notify banks and financial institutions.
5. Business premises signage.
6. All government registrations and licences.
7. Share certificates – Issue new certificates or endorse existing ones.

## Restrictions on Company Names
- Must end in "Limited" (English) or "有限公司" (Chinese), or both.
- Cannot be identical to an existing registered name.
- Cannot be misleading (e.g., suggesting connection to the government).
- Certain words require approval or a licence (e.g., "bank", "insurance", "trust", "royal").
- The Registrar has discretion to refuse names that are offensive or contrary to public interest.

## Chinese and English Names
- A company may register both an English and Chinese name.
- The English and Chinese names are treated as separate names; both can be changed independently.
- File Form NNC2 for each language name being changed.

## Fees
- Application fee: HKD 295

## Relevant Legislation
- Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622), Sections 107–120
