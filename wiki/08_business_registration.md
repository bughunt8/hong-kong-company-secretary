# Business Registration in Hong Kong

## Overview
Any person or company carrying on business in Hong Kong must obtain a Business Registration Certificate (BRC) from the Inland Revenue Department (IRD). This is separate from and in addition to company incorporation with the Companies Registry.

## Legal Requirement
- Required under the Business Registration Ordinance (Cap. 310).
- Must be obtained within 1 month of commencing business.
- Must be displayed prominently at the place of business.
- Applies to: companies incorporated in Hong Kong, foreign companies registered in Hong Kong, sole proprietorships, partnerships.

## Business Registration Certificate (BRC)
The BRC shows:
- Business Registration Number (BRN) – unique identifier for the business.
- Business name.
- Nature of business.
- Business address.
- Validity period (1 year or 3 years).

## Registration Process for New Companies
- When a new company is incorporated via the Companies Registry, a BRC is issued simultaneously (one-stop service).
- The BRN is the same as the Company Registration Number (CR Number).

## Renewal of BRC
- 1-year BRC: HKD 2,150 per year (subject to change).
- 3-year BRC: HKD 5,950 (subject to change).
- Renewal notice sent by IRD about 1 month before expiry.
- Can renew online via eTAX or at IRD offices.
- Late renewal penalty: Additional HKD 435 (or higher for extended late period).

## Change of Business Particulars
- Notify IRD within 1 month of any change to:
  - Business name
  - Business address
  - Nature of business
  - Other registered particulars
- File using Form IRC3111B (for sole proprietor/partnership) or via eCR (for companies).

## Business Registration Number (BRN)
- The BRN is an 8-digit number assigned by the IRD.
- Used on all official correspondence, invoices, and tax filings.
- Public can verify BRN at IRD's Business Registration Search System.

## Branch Registration
- If a company opens additional business locations, each branch may need to be registered separately.
- Branch registration fee applies.

## Cessation of Business
- Must notify IRD within 1 month of ceasing business.
- File Form IRC3111C.

## Relevant Legislation
- Business Registration Ordinance (Cap. 310)
