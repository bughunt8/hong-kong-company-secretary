# Directors and Officers in Hong Kong Companies

## Overview
A Hong Kong private company must have at least one director who is a natural person. Directors are responsible for managing the company's business and affairs.

## Director Requirements
- **Minimum**: 1 director who must be a natural person (individual), not a corporate entity.
- **Nationality/Residency**: No restrictions – directors can be of any nationality and reside anywhere in the world.
- **Age**: Must be at least 18 years old.
- **Disqualification**: Undischarged bankrupts and persons disqualified by court order cannot be directors.

## Types of Directors
- **Executive Director** – Involved in day-to-day management.
- **Non-Executive Director (NED)** – Provides oversight without day-to-day involvement.
- **Independent Non-Executive Director (INED)** – Required for listed companies; no material relationship with the company.
- **Alternate Director** – Appointed by a director to act in their place during absence.
- **Shadow Director** – Person whose instructions the board is accustomed to act upon (has director duties even without formal appointment).

## Director Duties (Companies Ordinance Cap. 622, Part 11)
1. **Duty to act in good faith** – Act in the best interests of the company.
2. **Duty to act for proper purpose** – Exercise powers only for the purposes for which they were conferred.
3. **Duty to avoid conflicts of interest** – Disclose interests in transactions.
4. **Duty of care, skill and diligence** – Act with reasonable care and competence.
5. **Duty not to accept benefits from third parties**.
6. **Duty to declare interest in proposed transactions**.

## Appointment of Directors
- First directors: Named in Incorporation Form (NNC1).
- Subsequent directors: Board resolution + filing Form ND2A with Companies Registry within 15 days.

## Removal of Directors
- By shareholder ordinary resolution (Companies Ordinance Section 460).
- Filing Form ND2A within 15 days of change.

## Register of Directors
- Must be maintained as a statutory register.
- Available for public inspection at Companies Registry.
- Includes: full name, residential/correspondence address, ID/passport details, date of appointment/cessation.

## Officers
- **Company Officers** include directors and the company secretary.
- Managers may also be classified as officers in certain contexts.
- Officers may be personally liable for company offences if committed with their consent or connivance.

## Residential Address Protection
- Directors may apply to protect their residential address from public disclosure.
- A correspondence/service address can be registered instead.
- Application via Form NR3.

## Relevant Legislation
- Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622), Parts 10 and 11
- Companies (Directors' Report) Regulation
